# Citation rules

Every material factual claim cites source IDs in the same result. Cite only successful fetches and preserve their URLs.
Mark interpretation as inference. Never invent metadata, quotations, or IDs.

Source and claim IDs are stable and unique; supporting/opposing references must close over the result source set.
Insufficient support requires `unverified=true`, a consequential limitation, and an unresolved/unverified item. Put
source role, independence, sample/method limits, and what it cannot prove in `limitations`; relationships stay in
`supportingSourceIds`/`opposingSourceIds`. “Insufficient” is not “refuted”; no located counterevidence is not proof.

Optional quotes are at most 25 English words or 80 CJK characters per source. Prefer paraphrase; never reconstruct a
page from excerpts or exceed bounds for paywalled text or lyrics.
