# Source quality

Assign roles during research without adding a schema field:

| Role | Can establish | Cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim Origin | a party made a promotion/promise | that it is true |
| Primary Evidence | original facts in docs, papers, code, standards, rules, prices, or terms | independent effects/universal conclusions |
| Independent Validation | findings from an unaffiliated test, dataset, or reproducible method | beyond its sample/method |
| Counterevidence | failures, limits, conflicts, retractions, inconsistent positions | the complete opposite |
| Context | terms and history | direct material-claim verification |

Treat snippets as discovery only. Capture publisher, title, URL, date/status, relevance, independence, and method limits.
Search rank, popularity, and syndicated copies are not independent corroboration.

When feasible, a material judgment needs primary plus independent evidence, or two independent authoritative sources.
Prefer papers/code/model cards/standards for technical origin; reproducible independent tests for capability;
prices/terms/disclosures/transactions for commerce; effective official texts and enforcement material for policy. A
vendor page proves only its claim exists. Without independent support conclude
"promotion verified; truth not independently verified"; never invent evidence.

Normalize/deduplicate canonical locators. Let shared Web policy enforce URL/network safety. Preserve credible conflicts,
scope, staleness, missing dates, and unresolved reasons; none may silently verify a claim. Evidence strength is only
high/medium/low, never a pseudo-precise probability. Fetch the smallest useful body and paraphrase within copyright
bounds.
